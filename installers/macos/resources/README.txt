@NAME@ @VERSION@

A subharmonic generator and sub-bass enhancer plug-in. This installer puts the VST3 in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
and the Audio Unit in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components. It needs macOS 11 or newer and runs natively on Apple
silicon and Intel Macs.

This is a development preview: the plug-in has a plain placeholder interface and no factory presets yet.

The installer is not signed. If macOS refuses to open it, open System Settings > Privacy & Security and click
"Open Anyway" next to the message about it.

To remove @NAME@, run in Terminal:
    sudo "/Library/Application Support/@VENDOR@/@NAME@/uninstall.sh"
